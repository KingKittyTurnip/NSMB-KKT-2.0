New Bobomb Moveset:
-a good deal slower than mario
-low jump height
-bobombs can triple jump anywhere, giving significant jump boosts
-Ignight, upon ignighting you pause for a few frames and get flinged in the direction you want, you can barely move during this (you cannot explode during the pause)
  -touching a bobomb that is lit will cause you both to explode
  -touching an unlit bobomb will make you bounce off them
  -touching a player will kill them directly
-hitting a bobomb lowers time by one * bobomb count

-very slow, speed increases slowly overtime
-sluggish but high and low grav jump
-ignight and charge up for a large explosion
  -destroyed every object in the game
  -upon respawning it's just a little bomb
-hitting king bobomb with a projectile lowers time by 3 * bobomb count
